- Every player will get an individual mob only they can see that is killable
  - Damage can be dealt automatically with DPS (Damage per second)
  - Damage can be dealt manually by clicking the mob (% of DPS)
    - Auto clickers will be allowed
    - CPS (Clicks per second) will be capped to a certain amount
      - Can be increased by having a rank
      - Can be increased through perks (prestige)
    - % of DPS increasable through perks/ranks
- The entire progression is phase based on the primary mob
  - Every x mobs you kill, a boss will appear
    - Bosses MUST be killed within x time to allow you progression into the next phase
    - If you do not kill a boss, you will reset the phase and continue farming resources until you can beat the phase
    - Bosses can drop significantly better loot such as blueprints or boxes
- Resources are the core of the game loop
  - Collected from killing the mob/bosses, boxes, rank rewards, generators etc...
  - Can be crafted into other resources which have various attributes, primarily DPS
  - Config driven, reloadable at any time for additions or changes (Previously was hard coded)
- Every player can have up to 5 hero slots
  - Heros are NPCS that can deal additional damage or buffs to the mob
  - Slots are unlocked through game progression, rank bonuses and crate rewards (very very rare)
  - Heros can level up but its limited to 1 at a time (Monetization space here for rank bonuses)
- Every player can have up to x generators unlocked
  - Generators can be setup to automatically craft resources
    - Blueprints can be found and when put in a generator, allows players to create that resource over some time
    - Blueprints have limited uses
    - Blueprints are config driven like resources, can be updated on the fly
  - Generators can be permanent or temporary
    - Permanent are unlocked through progression + ranks
    - Temporary come through crate rewards, time is stackable and stop when nothing is generating
- Players can unlock special activatable abilities through game progression
- Players can prestige to help them progress via upgrades
  - A ideas implemented in the old code already, bring those over and offer more
  - We can do upgrades such as:
    - % chance increase on box drops when boss is killed
- Boxes (In sennetfishing, they are crates i belive)
  - Tiered boxes that can be obtained through crates/killing the boss mobs/daily rank rewards
  - Boxes can be merged together in bulk to create higher tier boxes
  - Boxes offer 2 types of rewards
    - Guaranteed rewards when opening the box you will always get
    - "1 of" rewards which you will have a chance to receive from a pool of rewards
  - Boxes can contain resources directly, blueprints and at the highest tiers may offer chance for temporary ranks, hero slots, generator time
- Offline rewards
  - After x minutes offline, you will be able to collect offline rewards up to a certain amount of time
  - Time increasable through ranks, claimable tickets (from boxes or crates)
  - Time recharges as you are online and actively playing the game
- Global goals
  - I want to have global goals that players can contribute to unlock features or temporary boosts etc...
  - Can be stuff like forfeiting resources (limited per person)
- Leaderboards & competition
  - Weekly/Monthly/All time
    - Most mob kills
    - Highest DPS
    - Highest Phase
  - Payouts/rewards for top players like store credit high tier boxes or unique blueprints
- Player vaults for storage
  - Unlock more vaults + rows through ranks and vouchers
  - Enderchest is always unlocked for players as default
