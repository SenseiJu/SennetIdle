SENNETIDLE - FEATURES
=====================

OVERVIEW
--------
SennetIdle is an idle clicker game server. Every player fights their own mob in
the middle of spawn. The mob takes damage on its own over time, and players can
click it to deal extra damage. Killing mobs moves you up waves, gives you money
and drops resources. You use those resources to craft weapons that make you
stronger. Once you reach a high enough wave you can promote (prestige), which
resets your wave but unlocks new content and gives you upgrade points.


IDLE MOBS
---------
Your own personal mob that you fight forever.

- Every player gets their own mob at the spawn point
- Other players can't see your mob, and you can't see theirs
- Players standing near the mob are hidden from each other so they don't block clicks
- The mob loses health every second based on your DPS (damage per second)
- Clicking the mob deals extra damage (20% of your DPS per hit)
- Mob health goes up by 15% every wave
- Normal mobs are random: zombies, skeletons, blazes, spiders, creepers, endermen and more
- You get $15 for every hit and $100 for every kill
- A health bar at the top of the screen shows the mob's health


WAVES
-----
How far you have progressed.

- Every mob you kill moves you up one wave
- Higher waves mean tougher mobs and bigger resource drops
- Some resources only start dropping once you reach a certain wave
- Holograms around the mob show your wave, mob health, clicks per second and DPS


BOSS WAVES
----------
A tougher fight every 10 waves.

- Every 10th wave is a boss (Iron Golem or Warden)
- The sky turns to night and a "BOSS WAVE" title appears
- You only have 10 seconds to kill it, shown by a timer bar
- Some weapons only deal damage to bosses
- If you fail, you drop back 9 waves


RESOURCES
---------
Items you collect and craft to get stronger.

- Raw resources drop from mobs: Wood, String, Stone, Gold, Iron, Diamond, Netherite, Nebula
- Drops get bigger the further you are into waves
- Raw resources are crafted into parts (handles, blades) and then into weapons
- Weapons include swords, bows, wands, staffs and toy soldiers
- Every weapon you own adds DPS, so owning more of them makes you stronger
- Some resources are locked until you reach a certain number of promotions
- /resources opens the menu showing everything you own and what's locked
- Clicking a craftable item opens the crafting menu
- You can craft 1, 4, 16, 64 or 256 at a time
- Note: about half of the resources are placeholders and still need finishing


UPGRADES
--------
Permanent boosts you buy with upgrade points.

- Upgrade points come from promoting (3 per promotion)
- Each upgrade costs 1 point per level
- Sharpness: more click damage (+5% per level, max 25)
- Critical: chance to deal double click damage (+1.5% per level, max 10)
- Harvester: chance to drop extra resources on hit (not working yet, max 10)
- Explosive: chance to instantly kill a normal mob (+1% per level, max 25)
- /upgrades opens the menu, /upgrades balance shows your points


POWERS
------
Abilities on your hotbar that you right-click to use.

- Fireball: instantly deals 10% of the mob's max health (45 second cooldown)
- Adrenaline Rush: 15% more click damage for 5 seconds (2 minute cooldown)
- Powers are given to you automatically when you join
- Your inventory is locked so you can't move or drop them


PROMOTIONS
----------
The prestige system.

- First promotion needs wave 51, then 30 more waves for each one after that
- Promoting resets your wave back to 1
- Each promotion gives you 3 upgrade points
- Promotions unlock new resources
- You get a message when you're ready to promote
- /promote opens the promotion menu


NPCS
----
Clickable NPCs around spawn.

- Uses FancyNpcs
- NPCs can open the resources menu or the upgrades menu
- Which NPCs do what is set by name in config.yml


PLACEHOLDERS
------------
Stats you can show on scoreboards, tab lists and holograms (PlaceholderAPI).

- %idle_user_current_wave% - your current wave
- %idle_user_promotions% - how many times you have promoted
- %idle_user_dps% - your damage per second
- %idle_user_mob_health% - your mob's health, like 1.2K/5K
- %idle_user_cps% - your clicks per second


SAVING
------
Player progress is kept in a database.

- Uses MySQL / MariaDB
- Saves your wave, promotions, upgrade points, resources and upgrades
- Auto saves every 2 minutes (changeable in config.yml) and when the server stops


ADMIN COMMANDS
--------------
- /resources give <player> <resource> <amount> - give a player resources
- /upgrades addUpgradePoints <player> <amount> - give a player upgrade points
- /upgrades set <player> <upgrade> <level> - set a player's upgrade level
- Permissions: idle.resource.gui, idle.resource.modify, idle.upgrade.gui, idle.upgrade.modify
